About Tamayah

Who we are.

Tamayah is a specialized provider of medical claims management and settlement solutions, operating under the supervision and oversight of the Insurance Authority.

About Tamayah

We bring together advanced technology, specialized expertise and operational excellence to simplify and streamline the medical claims lifecycle. From medical authorization management and claims processing to settlement and financial reconciliation, our solutions are designed to deliver greater efficiency, accuracy, transparency and speed.

By combining intelligent digital solutions with experienced operational teams, we help our partners optimize claims operations, enhance service quality and maintain compliance with applicable regulatory and professional standards.

Regulatory standing

Tamayah operates under the supervision and oversight of the Insurance Authority and is authorized to conduct transactions through the Nphies platform.

Our vision

To be the region’s most trusted health benefits administrator, where every riyal spent buys better health.

Our mission

We give insurers, self-funded employers and health systems the technology, network and expertise to run their health programs efficiently, transparently and at scale.

Our commitment

We are committed to continuously improving the way healthcare claims and benefits are managed by combining specialized expertise, operational excellence and advanced technology.

Through continuous development and operational excellence, we aim to help our partners achieve greater efficiency while delivering a smoother and more reliable claims experience.
