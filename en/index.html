What we do

One connected claims-and-benefits ecosystem.

At Tamayah, we manage the healthcare claims and benefits administration journey through an integrated approach that combines technology, expertise and operational discipline. Our services support partners throughout the claims lifecycle, enabling efficient processing, accurate decision-making and transparent settlement.

  1. 01

    Medical Authorizations

    We manage and review medical authorization requests efficiently and accurately, ensuring alignment with applicable coverage benefits, policy terms and conditions, and regulatory requirements.

  2. 02

    Medical Claims Management

    We receive, review, validate and process medical claims through advanced technology-enabled systems, helping ensure accuracy, efficiency and timely resolution throughout the claims lifecycle.

  3. 03

    Claims Settlement & Reconciliation

    We manage claims settlement and financial reconciliation processes, identify and address discrepancies, and facilitate accurate and transparent claim closure.
